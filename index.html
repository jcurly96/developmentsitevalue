The Land Value Gap

Two Ways To Value The Same Property

What Rent Tells You

Income-based valuation using NOI and cap rates. This is what a buyer would pay for the cash flow the building generates today.

What Zoning Tells You

Land-based valuation using FAR (floor area ratio) and development comparables. This is what a buyer would pay for the unbuilt potential.

Is Your Property Under-Built?

Signs Your Site May Be Worth More As Land

✓
Your building is only 2 to 4 stories tall, even though local zoning allows buildings of 8 to 20 stories.
✓
Neighboring parcels have recently sold to buyers planning new construction.
✓
Your existing structure is primarily office or commercial space rather than residential.
✓
Current tenants are month to month, market rate, or the space sits empty.
✓
A recent rezoning or upzoning increased the buildable potential nearby.
✓
Your lot sits on a corner, is larger than typical, or borders parcels that could be assembled together.
